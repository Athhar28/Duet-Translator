DUET TRANSLATOR — Chrome / Edge extension
=========================================

INSTALL (one time, about 1 minute)
1. Unzip Duet_Translator_Extension.zip. You get a folder called "duet-ext".
2. Chrome: open  chrome://extensions
   Edge:   open  edge://extensions
3. Turn ON "Developer mode" (top-right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and select the "duet-ext" folder.
5. Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Duet Translator.
   Keep the folder where it is; Chrome loads the extension from it.

OPEN IT
- Click the Duet icon in the toolbar (or press Alt+Shift+T). It opens in the side panel.
- The first time you tap a mic, a tab opens asking for microphone access. Click Allow, close that tab.

FACE-TO-FACE OR PHONE ON SPEAKER
- Pick your language (bottom) and theirs (top).
- Tap YOUR mic and speak, or THEIR mic when they speak. Each translation shows on the other side
  and is read aloud (turn off with "Speak aloud").
- You can also type in either box.

ONLINE MEETINGS (Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom in the browser)
1. Join the meeting in Chrome/Edge (not the desktop app).
   If the meeting tab was open before you installed the extension, reload it.
2. Turn on captions inside the meeting and set the caption/spoken language to the other
   person's language.
3. In the Duet side panel, press "Meeting captions".
4. What the other person says is translated into your language as captions arrive.
5. To answer: tap your mic (or type), then press "Copy" on the translation and paste it into the
   meeting chat, or read it out.

NOTES
- Needs internet (speech recognition and translation are online services).
- Read-aloud uses the voices installed on your computer. If a language stays silent, add that
  language's speech voice in Windows Settings > Time & Language > Speech.
- Meeting apps change their page layout from time to time. If captions stop being picked up,
  the face-to-face mics and typing still work.
